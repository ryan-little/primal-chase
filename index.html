Learn to Run

You are an apex predator — a big cat built for explosive speed. The humans behind you are built for something else entirely. They track. They follow. They never stop. This is persistence hunting, and you cannot win. You can only survive one more day.

THE MAP IS THE CHOICE

Each phase you pick where to go. Highlighted hexes are within reach. One hex is a Trot. Two hexes is a Push — twice the ground, far more of your body spent. Staying put lets you Rest, drink, or hunt.

THE GROUND REMEMBERS

Sand and clay hold your prints like a confession. Bare rock and running water hold nothing. Cross ground that keeps your secret and the pursuit can break entirely — but each time they find your trail again, they come back faster.

THE CYCLE

Two phases a day: DAY and NIGHT. Days are brutal — heat builds and everything costs more. At night the hunters make camp and their fire is visible for miles. Use the darkness.

YOUR VITALS

  • Heat — Builds when you run. Heatstroke kills. Rest to cool.
  • Fatigue — Your exhaustion. Rest to recover.
  • Thirst — Rises every phase. Find water or dehydrate.
  • Hunger — Rises slowly. You can always try to hunt.

READING THE LAND

  • Water — Blue hexes. Drink, and hide your trail.
  • Stone — Hard going, but it takes no print.
  • Thicket — Slows you. Slows them more.
  • Landmarks — Marked with a light. Something waits there.

CONTROLS

  • Tap or click a hex to plan, again to commit
  • Drag to orbit · pinch or scroll to zoom
  • 1–6 actions · QE rotate · L look back
  • Space confirm · Esc menu
