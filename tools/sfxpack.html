Every cue has a BEFORE (the engine as it was, tools/audio-before.js) and an AFTER (live js/audio.js). Each click plays a fresh take, so you hear the variation. The picture is one offline render: a spectrogram on a log-frequency axis (40 Hz to 16 kHz, bright is loud) with the level envelope above it; click it to play that exact render. Numbers: integrated loudness (LUFS, K-weighted over the audible span), peak (dBFS), length. Positional cues are shown at full volume, centre.
